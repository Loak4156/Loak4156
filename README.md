## About

I connect **threat research, intelligence operations, and engineering** to help analysts make better decisions. My work spans ransomware, dark web intelligence, OSINT, and data breach analysis — with a particular focus on Russian-language open and closed sources.

With **6+ years in cybersecurity**, I have moved from leading SOC operations to building and operationalizing threat intelligence capabilities. Previously, I led a **20-analyst Tier 1 SOC team (2020–2024)**. That operational background shapes how I build: useful context, clear priorities, and workflows analysts can actually use.

## CyberTI

### Threat Intelligence Operations Platform

My current focus: building a CTI platform that brings **dark web, ransomware leak sites, data breaches, forums, RSS, and social intelligence** into one operational workflow.

```text
  COLLECT                 CORRELATE                OPERATIONALIZE
  ───────                 ─────────                ──────────────
  Dark web & forums  ─┐
  Ransomware feeds   ─┼──▶ IOC extraction     ───▶ Asset-aware triage
  Breach signals    ─┤    Deduplication            Analyst review
  RSS & social      ─┘    Context enrichment       Response
```

Built around real CTI requirements: reduce repetitive work, preserve useful context, and help analysts move from a signal to a decision.
